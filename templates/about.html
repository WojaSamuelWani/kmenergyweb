COMPANY OVERVIEW

One company.
Multiple capabilities.

K&M Energy Group Ltd is a registered and legally compliant energy solutions provider in South Sudan. Its core business includes mobile and door-to-door fuel delivery, helping clients reduce the time and logistics involved in refuelling vehicles, generators and equipment.

The company profile also presents services in oil and gas, freight and logistics, car hire, building materials, construction, ICT equipment, electrical supplies, furniture, agro trading, foodstuffs and safety equipment.
